NOW ENROLLING KIDS IN THE FOLLOWING CLASSES

Juniors

Ages: 7-12

Junior martial arts is a specialized program created exclusively for children ages 7 through 12. The foundation of the kids martial arts curriculum is the ongoing review and development of life skills such as:

Positive mental attitude • High goal-setting • Perseverance • Self-control

They will learn to work with others, to control their mindset, say Yes Sir and Yes Ma'am, and take responsibility for themselves and their actions. In addition, they will know how to protect themselves in a potentially dangerous situation.

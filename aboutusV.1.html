WHAT IS JEET KUNE DO

Jeet Kune Do, founded by Bruce Lee, is more than a martial art it’s a philosophy of adaptability and self expression. It is not bound by rigid techniques but guided by what truly works. At American Dragon, one of only three schools in Florida preserving Bruce Lee’s lineage through Dan Inosanto, we bring this philosophy into every lesson. We don’t train children to imitate others we help them discover their own unique strengths and style.

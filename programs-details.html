What is Jeet Kune Do?

Decades before anyone ever heard of "Mixed Martial Arts", the legendary Bruce Lee developed a revolutionary martial art training process, which combined different aspects, principles, and techniques of various Martial Arts styles.

Bruce Lee named his process “Jeet Kune Do”, and is the foundation of our teaching at American Dragon Martial Arts Academy.

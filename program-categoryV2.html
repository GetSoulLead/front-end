NOW ENROLLING KIDS IN THE FOLLOWING CLASSES

Juniors

Ages: 7-12

Junior martial arts is a specialized program created exclusively for children ages 7 through 12. The foundation of the kids martial arts curriculum is the ongoing review and development of life skills such as:

Positive mental attitude • High goal-setting • Perseverance • Self-control

They will learn to work with others, to control their mindset, say Yes Sir and Yes Ma'am, and take responsibility for themselves and their actions. In addition, they will know how to protect themselves in a potentially dangerous situation.

Teens

Ages: 7-12

Advanced martial arts program for teenagers ages 13 through 17. Focus on leadership development and advanced techniques.

Leadership skills • Advanced techniques • Strategic thinking • Confidence building

Develop real-world leadership skills, advanced self-defense techniques, and mental toughness.

Little Dragons

Ages: 4-6

Fun introductory program for young children ages 4 through 6. Focus on motor skills, listening, and confidence building through games.

Motor skills • Listening skills • Coordination • Confidence

Learn through play! Develop essential motor skills, listening, following directions, and building confidence.
